🧤 HHIB Episode 5: Ms. Mitt Is Back — Case Closed?

The Harmony House Investigation Bureau (HHIB) finally has some very good news.

Ms. Mitt has been found! 🎉

After being missing for quite some time, the red oven mitt—twin sister of the other Ms. Mitt— suddenly turned up in a rather unexpected location:

Mr. Olive Oil’s apartment. 🫒

Naturally, this looked suspicious.

But HHIB quickly established one important fact: Mr. Olive Oil was not there at the time.

Therefore, HHIB has no reason to suspect Mr. Olive Oil of having anything to do with Ms. Mitt’s disappearance.

Mr. Olive Oil is cleared. ✅

That leaves just one unanswered question.

🔎 How Did Ms. Mitt Get There?

Ms. Mitt is not known to walk around Harmony House by herself. Somehow, somebody must have helped her travel to Mr. Olive Oil’s apartment.

HHIB currently has two possible explanations.

Possibility #1: Mrs. Cleaning Lady.
Perhaps Ms. Mitt was accidentally picked up while Mrs. Cleaning Lady was working and was later left near Mr. Olive Oil.

Possibility #2: Mrs. Home Owner.
Mrs. Home Owner is known to move rather quickly around Harmony House. It is entirely possible that she carried Ms. Mitt somewhere, put her down near Mr. Olive Oil, and simply does not remember doing it.

Unfortunately, HHIB has found no evidence that can establish which explanation is correct.

And Ms. Mitt herself?

She is providing absolutely no useful information. 🧤

After careful consideration, HHIB has therefore decided that there is little more to investigate.

The most important fact is known: Ms. Mitt is safely home.

And so Dr. Z’s case notes will record:

Missing resident: FOUND. ✅
Mr. Olive Oil: CLEARED. ✅
Transportation to Mr. Olive Oil’s apartment: UNRESOLVED. ❓
Case status: CLOSED. 📁

Sometimes a case can be closed even when one small mystery refuses to go away.
