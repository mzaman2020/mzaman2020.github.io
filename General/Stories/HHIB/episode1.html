Harmony House Investigation Bureau (HHIB)

Episode 1: The Mystery of the Missing Red Oven Mitt

🌞 A Very Strange Morning

One bright morning, Ms. Mitt stretched her soft red arms and smiled.

Ms. Mitt: “Good morning, Sister!”

“Are you ready for another baking day?”

There was no reply.

Ms. Mitt looked beside the oven.

Nothing.

She looked inside the kitchen drawer.

Nothing.

She looked behind the bread pan.

Nothing.

She looked near the cooling rack.

Still nothing.

Her twin sister was nowhere to be found.

Ms. Mitt: “Oh dear. This has never happened before.”

Ms. Mitt took a deep breath.

Then she called the Harmony House Investigation Bureau — HHIB.

🕵️ Chief Inspector Arrives

A few minutes later...

Knock! Knock!

Chief Inspector Dr. Z arrived carrying a small detective notebook and a warm smile.

Dr. Z: “Good morning, Ms. Mitt.”

“Tell me everything from the beginning.”

Ms. Mitt looked worried.

Ms. Mitt: “My twin has disappeared.”

“I don't know where she went.”

“Do you think something bad happened?”

Dr. Z: “Maybe yes. Maybe no.”

“A good detective never guesses first.”

“We observe first...”

“We think second...”

“And we decide last.”

🔎 Looking for Clues

The investigation began.

Together, Dr. Z and Ms. Mitt searched the kitchen.

  • They checked the cupboard.
  • They searched the baking shelf.
  • They looked near the flour container.
  • They checked the bread basket.
  • They opened every drawer.

Everything looked neat and tidy.

Fresh banana bread had recently been baked.

But only one red oven mitt remained.

Dr. Z: “Interesting...”

“One mitten is missing, but nothing else seems unusual.”

“Every clue matters.”

👩‍🍳 Visiting Ms. Red Apron

Suddenly, Ms. Mitt had an idea.

Ms. Mitt: “Perhaps Ms. Red Apron knows something!”

Dr. Z: “Excellent thinking.”

So they went together to visit Ms. Red Apron.

Ms. Mitt: “Have you seen my sister today?”

Ms. Red Apron: “I'm sorry. I haven't seen her today.”

“But if I notice anything, I promise to tell HHIB.”

Dr. Z: “Every witness helps, even when they don't know the answer.”

🌟 The First HHIB Rule

Before leaving, Dr. Z closed the detective notebook.

Dr. Z: “Stay calm.”

“Observe carefully.”

“Think kindly.”

“Never jump to conclusions.”

Ms. Mitt smiled.

She still missed her twin sister.

But now she also had hope.

❓ The Mystery Continues

The mystery is still unsolved.

Nobody knows where the missing twin is.

Not Ms. Mitt.

Not Dr. Z.

Not even HHIB.
